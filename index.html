Fantasy football, but you draft a fresh roster each week.

FantasyX is a full-stack fantasy football platform where every league re-drafts each week. Once you start a player, they enter your graveyard and leave your draft pool for the season, making every pick matter.

I designed and built the Flutter client, Postgres architecture, realtime draft engine, scoring pipeline, 30 edge functions, and release workflow.
