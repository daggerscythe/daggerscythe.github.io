Win the Week. Survive the Season.

A shipped, full-stack fantasy football platform built on a mechanic no other fantasy app has: every league re-drafts its entire roster every single week. Start a player and let his game play, and he is buried in your personal graveyard, locked out of your draft pool for the rest of the season. The pool thins as the season goes on, so every pick carries a permanent cost.

I am a co-founder and the sole engineer. I built the whole thing solo: the Flutter client for iOS, Android and web, the Postgres schema and its stored procedures, 30 serverless edge functions, the realtime live draft engine, the scoring pipeline, the App Store and Play compliance work, and the marketing site.

Flutter Dart Riverpod Supabase PostgreSQL Deno Python Firebase Cloudflare

Clam It! (2D)

This 2D management game was developed solo in 2 weeks as a project for CSCI 5499B - Game Development in Fall 2026. It was built in Godot 4.7 and programmed with GDScript. You play as a pearl farmer who plants, cleans, harvests, and sells pearls while defending them from crabs. Chum makes pearls grow faster but attracts crabs, so every click is a risk-vs-reward call, and a limited supply of sonic flares is the only way to scare them off. It has a guided tutorial stage that introduces one tool per level, a freeplay mode up to Level 30, 7 pearl types based on real-world pearls with weighted drop rates, an upgrade shop, and a save system. The game was balanced through several rounds of playtesting. All sprites (drawn in Piskel) and audio were made by me. Played with mouse and keyboard.
